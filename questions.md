# Client Questionnaire

Short answers are fine. "I don't know yet" is a real answer.

## A. The business

A1 In one or two sentences: what does your company do?
A2 How do you get paid — commission on shipped sales, monthly retainer, project fee, or a mix? (No numbers needed here. It decides whether the system needs commission math at all, which is the single biggest fork in what we build.)
A3 Which retailers and distributors do you sell into, and roughly how many brands do you represent right now? (Walmart, Sam's, Target, Kroger, Dollar General, HEB, Costco, UNFI, KeHE, McLane…)

## B. Who's in it

B1 Who would log in, and what is each person's role?
B2 Should anyone see only their own accounts, or be kept away from money — commissions, margins, what others earn?

## C. How the work actually runs

C1 Attach the spreadsheets and documents you run the business on today. If you'd rather not send them, describe what's in each. (This one is worth more than the rest of the form put together — it shows us how you already work instead of how you'd describe it.)
C2 Walk a deal from first conversation to product on the shelf. Name each stage the way you'd say it out loud.
C3 How do line reviews and modular resets drive your calendar, and how far ahead do you start?
C4 What falls through the cracks most often? Tell us about the last one that cost you.

## D. Money

D1 How is commission calculated, and when do you actually get paid? (Ballpark is fine for now — we'll get exact before we build. We're after the shape of the rule: % of shipped invoice, per unit, tiered, different per client or retailer, and any exception you'd have to remember.)
D2 What number do you most want to see the second you log in?

## E. Ground rules

E1 What software do you use today, and which of it is untouchable? (Retail Link, Luminate, NIQ/Circana, Excel, QuickBooks, Google Workspace or Outlook, Dropbox, DocuSign, EDI providers)
E2 Have you tried a CRM before? Which one, and what specifically made you stop using it?
E3 When do you need this live, and is there a date that actually matters — a line review, a trade show, the fiscal year?
E4 If the first version only did three things, and did them perfectly, which three?

## W. If you also want a website (optional)

Skip this whole section if a website isn't part of this — nothing here affects the CRM.

W1 Do you already own a domain, and who has the login for it? Is anything live there today?
W2 If a visitor does exactly one thing on the site, what should it be — call you, fill out a form, download a line sheet, book a meeting?
W3 Should leads from the site's contact form land straight in the CRM as a new record, and who gets pinged when one comes in?
