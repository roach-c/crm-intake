# Client Questionnaire

Short answers are fine. "I don't know yet" is a real answer.
★ = if you only have 15 minutes, answer these.

## A. The business

A1 ★ In one or two sentences: what does your company do?
A2 ★ How do you get paid — commission on shipped sales, monthly retainer, project fee, or a mix? (No numbers needed here. It decides whether the system needs commission math at all, which is the single biggest fork in what we build.)
A3 How many brands/suppliers are you working with right now? How many in two years?
A4 ★ Which retailers and distributors do you sell into? (Walmart, Sam's, Target, Kroger, Dollar General, HEB, Costco, UNFI, KeHE, McLane, C&S…)
A5 Which categories/departments do you live in, and does the process differ between them?

## B. Who's in it

B1 ★ List everyone who would log in, with their role. Who is the owner/admin?
B2 ★ Should anyone see only their own accounts? Who is allowed to see money — commissions, margins, what others earn?
B3 Where does the work happen — phone in the field, laptop at a buyer meeting, desk? Roughly what % is mobile?
B4 Would you ever want supplier clients to log in and see their own status?
B5 Do you use contractors/1099 reps whose access should turn off when an engagement ends?

## C. What you keep track of

C1 ★ Send us the spreadsheets and documents you run the business on today. If you can't send them, describe what's in each.
C2 ★ For a brand/supplier you represent, what do you need on file? (contacts, category, capacity, GS1/UPC ownership, certifications, insurance, EDI capability, freight terms, MOQs, lead times, supplier agreement status)
C3 ★ For an individual item/SKU, what fields matter? (UPC, case pack, case cost, unit cost, suggested retail, margin, dims/weight, shelf life, item # once assigned, setup status)
C4 For a retailer contact, what do you track? (buyer, replen manager, dept & category #, last contact, preferred channel, when they rotate out)
C5 What files pile up on a deal, and should they live in the system? (decks, spec sheets, photos, cut sheets, forecasts, sample tracking, signed agreements)
C6 What currently lives in your head, in text messages, or on a legal pad that should be somewhere everyone can see?

## D. How a deal moves

D1 ★ Walk a deal from first conversation to product on the shelf. Name each stage the way you'd say it out loud.
D2 What has to be true before a deal moves from one stage to the next?
D3 ★ Where do deals die most often, and why?
D4 ★ How do line reviews and modular resets drive your calendar? Fixed review windows per category? How far ahead do you start?
D5 After a buyer says yes, what happens next, and how much of it do you track? (item setup, supplier agreement, first PO, first ship, store count, replenishment, performance review)
D6 Typical time from first pitch to first PO? Longest one you've waited out?
D7 ★ What tasks happen on every single deal, in the same order, every time?

## E. Money

E1 ★ Exactly how is commission calculated and when do you get paid? (% of shipped invoice, per unit, tiered, different per client/retailer — write the real rule including exceptions)
E2 Should the system calculate and forecast commission, or just record what you were paid?
E3 Do you split commission with anyone? How is the split figured?
E4 Do you want to invoice clients out of this system, or does billing stay where it is?
E5 ★ What number do you most want to see the second you log in?

## F. Reminders and reporting

F1 ★ What should the system nag you about, and how — email, text, or a list waiting when you log in?
F2 What do you send clients today, how often, in what format? Who builds it and how long does it take?
F3 If you could only ever have one report, what would it show?
F4 ★ What falls through the cracks most often? Tell us about the last one that cost you.

## G. Systems and ground rules

G1 ★ What software do you use today, and which of it is untouchable? (Retail Link, Luminate, NIQ/Circana, Excel, QuickBooks, Google Workspace/Outlook, Dropbox, DocuSign, EDI providers)
G2 Should email and calendar flow into the CRM so activity logs itself? Which provider is your email on?
G3 Where does existing data live, roughly how many records, and does history need to come across?
G4 Any confidentiality obligations to design around — NDAs with brands, retailer data that can't be commingled between clients?
G5 ★ Have you tried a CRM before? Which one, and what specifically made you stop using it?
G6 ★ When do you need this live, and is there a date that actually matters — line review, trade show, fiscal year?
G7 What budget range are you working with — one-time build, monthly, or both?
G8 ★ If the first version only did three things, and did them perfectly, which three?

## W. If you also want a website (optional)

Skip this whole section if a website isn't part of this — nothing here affects the CRM.

W1 Do you already own a domain? Who has the login for it, and is anything live there today? (registrar, host, an old site nobody updates)
W2 If a visitor does exactly one thing on the site, what should it be — call you, fill out a form, download a line sheet, book a meeting?
W3 Which brands and retailers can you name and show publicly, and which are under NDA or otherwise off-limits?
W4 What already exists that we can use? (logo files, headshots, product photography, capabilities deck, case studies, testimonials, trade-show booth art)
W5 Who updates the site after launch — us, someone on your staff, or do you want to be able to edit it yourself without calling anybody?
W6 Should leads from the site's contact form land straight in the CRM as a new record, and who gets pinged when one comes in?
